# Complete deliverable checklist

This checklist captures the reusable requirements behind a creator alliance
marketing recap. The user's current instructions and data take precedence.

1. Identify every requested creator in the affiliate platform, including
   aliases and channel names. Resolve ambiguous matches with an account ID,
   channel, or other evidence. Include newly contracted creators later added
   to the roster; do not silently omit them.
2. For every creator-month, open every affiliate order detail and separately
   sum `Order Amount` and `Commissionable Volume`, regardless of commission
   status. Group by the displayed order date, reconcile adjacent date filters
   at month boundaries, and retain non-sensitive audit counts. Also collect
   referrals and orders. Continue the period as the user extends it; label the
   snapshot date and avoid presenting a partial month as complete.
3. Collect creator-month content views from the authorized evaluation sheet.
   Preserve explicit zeros, absent rows, and not-yet-updated cells distinctly.
   Note any source caveat such as long-form-only counts.
4. Attach the listed monthly fee, agreement type, and effective date to each
   creator. Distinguish new signings from renewals. Show effective dates as
   annotations; do not gray out prior months merely because a listed contract
   date is later. Historical fees may differ from the current listed fee.
5. Evaluate each creator-month against the strict audited Order Amount > 2.5 x
   monthly-fee threshold. Highlight met and unmet outcomes in different
   colors, with text labels. Present Commissionable Volume separately and do
   not use it as the target numerator. Calculate referrals/content-views only
   where the denominator is recorded and positive.
6. Deliver a management-oriented English report with monthly analysis,
   creator/channel names, one consolidated comparison chart, a source and
   limitations section, and high-resolution PDF when requested. Keep raw
   chart assets in a private user-selected folder. Do not paste screenshots
   into the final report as a substitute for charts.
7. Deliver a publishable English website aligned with the user's own visual
   identity guide. It should expose a monthly creator matrix, switching among
   Order Amount, Commissionable Volume, Order Amount/fee, referrals, content
   views, referrals/views, and orders; search, agreement filtering, sorting,
   creator detail, and export of the reviewed view where authorized. Do not
   expose source credentials or private source-page URLs to public visitors
   unless explicitly approved.
8. For recurring updates, support separate schedules for affiliate/site data
   and content-view data, including weekly checks and every-other-Monday
   checks where requested. Run in the agreed timezone. Maintain a private
   successful-publication checkpoint and keep validated creator-month history
   locked. Read source pages directly only for newly completed months, newly
   added creators' uncollected months, and fields previously marked missing or
   not updated, without intermediate downloads or exports. Reopen a locked
   historical month only for an explicit correction or backfill request.
   Validate before writing, advance the checkpoint only after successful live
   readback, retain the prior publication and checkpoint on any failure, and
   disclose local-login or cloud-access limitations accurately.

No actual roster, account names, fee amounts, links, metrics, brand files, or
private report artifacts belong in this public skill.
